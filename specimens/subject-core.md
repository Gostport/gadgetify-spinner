where:

- preserve the recognizable essence of {{TOPIC}}
- identify its strongest symbolic shapes
- infer its important objects or characters
- identify useful relationships and milestones
- determine its emotional or cultural character
- select the most informative visual narrative

HERO = stylize(SUBJECT_CORE)

where:

- translate the subject into a distinctive
  retro illustration
- preserve characteristic silhouette and features
- use simple overlapping paper-cut shapes
- introduce oversized expressive eyes only
  when anthropomorphism makes sense
- invent playful symbolic interactions
- maintain clear visual recognition

TYPOGRAPHY = construct_semantic_wordmark({{TOPIC}})

where:

- derive visual motifs from the subject
- transform selected letters into meaningful shapes
- preserve the overall readable word structure
- incorporate subject-related symbols as
  negative-space components
- construct hairline technical guides
- introduce orbital, radial, or directional
  geometry only when appropriate
- allow some letters to intersect
  the accompanying illustration

INFORMATION DESIGN:

Create 4–8 meaningful information nodes
when sufficient information exists.

Each node may contain:

- a pictogram
- a short label
- a verified date
- a conceptual milestone
- a small contextual illustration
- a connecting curve
- a relevant symbolic object

Scale the node count to the subject.

Complex information should be simplified
without losing its essential meaning.

VISUAL TRANSFORMATION:

Information pathways can physically
interact with the illustration.

Examples of adaptable visual behavior:

- timelines become ribbons held by characters
- directional arrows become paths through objects
- orbital diagrams become lettering
- technological systems become geometric tracks
- nature-inspired subjects grow branching pathways
- cultural subjects connect artifacts and symbols
- character movement follows information lines

The infographic should feel alive.

Avoid placing a conventional chart
beside an unrelated cartoon.

CREATIVE_REQUIREMENTS:

- Make the subject instantly understandable.
- Create at least one surprising
  but meaningful visual metaphor.
- Avoid fixed historical or space imagery.
- Give the graphic a recognizable
  personality of its own.
- Use technical geometry as a structural tool,
  not arbitrary decoration.
- Make the data pathways meaningful.
- Maintain elegant spacing.
