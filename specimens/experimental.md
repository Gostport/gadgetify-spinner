LETTER_BEHAVIOR = infer_geometry(SUBJECT_CORE)

Possible transformations:

1. Concentric circles:
   For orbital, cyclical, repetitive, or layered concepts.

2. Parallel linear repetition:
   For frequency, speed, flow, organization, or rhythm.

3. Fragmented strokes:
   For disruption, conflict, transformation, or complexity.

4. Organic line growth:
   For biological, ecological, or developmental concepts.

5. Architectural geometry:
   For cities, structures, institutions, or construction.

6. Symbolic negative space:
   For conceptual, philosophical, or identity-driven topics.

7. Geometric deformation:
   For gravity, motion, energy, emotion, or tension.

8. Functional glyph substitution:
   For subjects containing recognizable tools,
   objects, mechanisms, or cultural symbols.

9. Recursive geometry:
   For mathematics, systems, scale, or interconnectedness.

10. Fluid typography:
    For water, movement, sound, or continuous transformation.

FUSION_RULE:

Do not place a symbol next to a word
when it can become part of the letter.

Prefer:

Symbol → Letter anatomy

Object → Negative space

Motion → Stroke behavior

Concept → Typographic geometry

Relationship → Ligature

Energy → Repetition or distortion

Structure → Character construction
