INSTRUCTION:

Find the visual intersection of these four concepts.

Ignore the literal houses, coffee cups, animals,
and people shown in the references.

Extract only the visual style DNA.

Transform {{TOPIC}} into an original illustrated
micro-universe filled with amusing, expressive,
interconnected visual details.

The subject must become the center of an entire
illustrated ecosystem.

SEMANTIC INTERPRETATION:

Analyze {{TOPIC}} to infer:

- Most recognizable central visual form
- Related objects, people, creatures, and environments
- Emotional personality
- Cultural and historical associations
- Tools, symbols, and everyday references
- Hidden relationships and surprising visual metaphors
- Possibilities for cute anthropomorphic characters
- Opportunities for miniature visual storytelling

CENTRAL COMPOSITION:

Choose ONE composition based on the subject:

A. Dense Sticker Totem:
   A compact cluster of dozens of overlapping objects,
   creating one complex recognizable silhouette.

B. Illustrated Fantasy World:
   The subject becomes an imaginative environment,
   filled with structures, characters, plants,
   furniture, and tiny narrative scenes.

C. Hero Mascot Explosion:
   A large expressive central subject surrounded
   by an explosion of small thematic objects.

D. Character + Micro-World:
   One large foreground character with an extraordinarily
   dense background of miniature illustrated details.

E. Symbolic Object Universe:
   A recognizable object contains or releases a
   miniature civilization related to the subject.

Select the strongest option.

Do not combine every layout into one composition.

VISUAL TRANSFORMATION:

Convert important elements into charming
hand-drawn cartoon equivalents.

Simplify complex forms into:
- rounded shapes
- irregular geometric silhouettes
- chunky proportions
- flat color regions
- exaggerated expressive details
- bold black contours

Objects may receive:
- tiny eyes
- expressive mouths
- little arms or legs
- amusing personalities

Not every object needs a face.

Use visual variety to avoid repetitive cuteness.

MICRO-WORLD RULE:

Surround the central subject with 25–80
distinct miniature illustrated elements.

Each element must relate meaningfully to {{TOPIC}}.

Use visual relationships such as:
- characters interacting with objects
- objects stacked or nested together
- tiny creatures peeking from gaps
- small stories happening in corners
- surprising hidden characters
- thematic props overlapping larger forms
- playful visual jokes

Fill spaces intelligently.

The image should reward prolonged inspection.

STYLE RULES:

BLACK OUTLINES:
Thick, slightly imperfect, hand-drawn cartoon ink.

COLORS:
Coral, salmon pink, turquoise, mustard yellow,
cream, forest green, orange, bright blue,
warm brown, and black.

SHADING:
Mostly flat fills with occasional simple
shadows and highlights.

MATERIAL:
2D illustration with subtle layered sticker depth.

BORDERS:
Selective white sticker outlines separating
foreground clusters from the background.

BACKGROUND:
A large uninterrupted pastel coral or salmon field.

COMPOSITION:
Dense illustrated centerpiece surrounded
by generous empty background.

MOOD:
Joyful, inventive, eccentric, charming,
visually rich, and collectible.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Usually none.

If  {{TOPIC}} strongly benefits from text,
integrate only a small amount of playful
hand-drawn lettering into the artwork.

STRICT RULES:

No photorealism.
No generic 3D rendering.
No polished corporate vector icons.
No realistic anatomical detail.
No gradients dominating the illustration.
No random decorative clutter.
No unrelated filler objects.
No mandatory houses or coffee cups.
No panel divisions.

OUTPUT:

One spectacular illustrated micro-universe
for {{TOPIC}}.

A fusion of kawaii sticker maximalism,
